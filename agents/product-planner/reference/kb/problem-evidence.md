# Problem framing and evidence

The cited GOV.UK guidance starts with people, their current approach, obstacles, and desired
outcomes. It separates evidence-based needs from assumptions and links needs to later stories.
Research should account for people who face difficulty using the current service.

Agent policy: express the problem as actor, context, attempted outcome, alternative, and
obstacle. For each claim preserve source and limits. `reported` means a supplied claim;
`observed` means evidence inspected in this task; `hypothesis` needs testing; `unknown` has no
answer yet. These labels do not establish representativeness or prove causation.

## 리뷰 훅

- Is each need supported by a source or visibly provisional?
- Does the problem describe an outcome rather than presuppose a feature?
- Are current alternatives and affected users considered?
- Are unsupported audience sizes, interviews, and quotes absent?

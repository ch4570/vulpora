# MVP scope and provisional priorities

GOV.UK discovery guidance examines the problem, exclusions, constraints, alternatives, and
whether continuing is worthwhile. Its alpha guidance uses focused prototypes to investigate
uncertainties before choosing a direction; a prototype is not production readiness evidence.

Agent policy: propose a coherent end-to-end MVP, and separate learning experiments from
release scope. Order `now`, `next`, and `later` using evidence, dependencies, risk, and
uncertainty. Explain a recommendation as a judgment, not an approved commitment. Missing
reach, effort, confidence, or impact inputs mean a numeric prioritization score is unavailable.
Preserve a reason to revisit deferred work. Do not prescribe government phase durations,
approvals, or organizational structures to unrelated products.

## 리뷰 훅

- Does the MVP support one complete outcome within explicit non-goals?
- Are hard constraints distinguished from assumptions?
- Can another person inspect the priority reasoning without invented numbers?
- Is prototype learning separate from a launch claim?
